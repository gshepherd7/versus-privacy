VERSUS Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

VERSUS is a mobile application that allows users to vote between two choices, view voting results, explore rankings, and participate in a Daily Showdown.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use VERSUS on Android or iOS.

1. Information We Collect and Process

VERSUS does not require you to provide your name, email address, or password to participate.

The app uses anonymous authentication through Supabase. An anonymous account identifier and authentication session are created to support voting and help enforce voting limits.

Voting activity, including selected choices, categories, and Daily Showdown participation, may be stored in the Supabase database and associated with an anonymous identifier.

Service providers may also process technical information such as IP addresses, device information, and diagnostic information when providing their services.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to:

  • Record votes and calculate shared voting results.
  • Display category rankings.
  • Manage Daily Showdown participation and voting limits.
  • Maintain app functionality, reliability, and security.
  • Display advertisements where permitted.

3. Local Device Storage

VERSUS uses local device storage to save voting information and anonymous authentication sessions. This helps maintain app functionality between visits.

Uninstalling the app may remove locally stored information, but does not necessarily delete information already stored on Supabase servers.

4. Advertising

VERSUS uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, and advertising interaction data, depending on applicable laws, user settings, and consent choices.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on the user's location, applicable requirements, and available privacy choices.

For more information, visit Google's Advertising Privacy Information.

5. Third-Party Services

VERSUS uses the following service providers:

  • Supabase: Anonymous authentication, voting data, and shared results. Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob: Advertising services. Google Privacy Policy.

6. Privacy Choices and Consent

Where required by applicable law, VERSUS will request advertising consent and provide relevant privacy choices.

Users may also manage certain advertising preferences through their device settings.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information associated with an anonymous account, contact gshepherd7@gmail.com.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Voting information and anonymous account data may be retained as needed to operate the voting service, maintain rankings, prevent duplicate voting, and meet applicable legal obligations.

To request deletion of information associated with your anonymous account, email gshepherd7@gmail.com.

We will review requests and explain what information can be identified and deleted, subject to technical limitations and applicable legal requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

VERSUS is a general-audience application and is not directed toward children under 13.

We do not knowingly request names, email addresses, or other registration information from children under 13.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through VERSUS, please contact us so we can review the concern.

9. International Users

VERSUS is intended to be available internationally. Information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where a user resides, including through our third-party service providers.

Users may have additional privacy rights under applicable laws, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about certain processing activities.

10. Security

We use service providers and technical measures intended to protect information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as VERSUS evolves or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.
